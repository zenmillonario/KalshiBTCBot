## Causal integrity and measurement limitations

- Physical BTC/EMA9/EMA30 mountain identity and ancestral arcs persist across Kalshi contracts; original ticker, YES/NO ask, fee, time-to-close and settlement stay distinct economic identities.
- The 12:30–1:50 p.m. R11 research window was independently covered in Phase6 geometry. The later lower-Heist observation does not reset physical mountain history.

- The actual Command tests use synthetic books sourced from recorded ask values; **synthetic book freshness is not proof original exchange liquidity could have filled**. The candidate-owned positions only evolve in selected synthetic paths (12 flat frames, real Command protected winner sequence). There is no full chronological native order-book state, whole-market position replay, or defensible alternative historical P/L.

- Inherited R11 logging-hook tests contaminate each other when installed multiple times in one Python process. This test runner uses a fresh subprocess for each suite. The underlying hook idempotence issue remains open.
- Four territory descriptive labels BASE, DRAGON, MARKET HEIST and TRANSFER are not four autonomous strategic authorities. The as-of samples do not prove 90% coverage or predictive accuracy.

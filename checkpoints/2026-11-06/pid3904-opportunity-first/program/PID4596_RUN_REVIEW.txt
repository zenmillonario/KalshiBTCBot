WHY IT STOPPED

At 8:35:42.843 p.m., separately retained market logs show the next book/geometry
frame. Its Heist classification moved from MIXED to NEUTRAL. The adviser then
tried to assess a tactical return. In a bounded reconstruction, 34 prior response
pairs produced a conservative price response of 0.0. The model therefore left
its estimated exit price unavailable and returned:
TACTICAL_RESPONSE_OR_REMAINING_TIME_INSUFFICIENT.

That is an ordinary unavailable-estimate result. A later line nevertheless
attempted to divide the missing value to form a suggested entry price. Python
raised TypeError, and the engine stopped. The patch checks whether the estimate
is a finite number before calculating the suggestion. If it is unavailable,
the adviser retains its WAIT reason and emits no suggested price. A stale old
suggestion is removed. The proposed trade fields and Commander authority are
preserved.

## 1. Enter from current observed geometry

The former source let a slower three-minute direction lag obscure a new native EMA9 turn. Current arc recognition now uses agreeing six- and nine-second EMA9 movement plus actual observed prominence. The slower direction remains useful trend context. Recognition records its actual observation time; the earlier physical origin is not represented as knowledge the bot already had.

Completed native arcs and separately bounded slower comparison envelopes supply recent shape/duration context. A comparison envelope has no permission to enter, exit, renew a mission or change ownership. It cannot be counted twice with the same physical turn. Cold or interrupted pending history must requalify; valid completed references retain their existing bounded lifetime.

A mature EMA9 terminal or retest can qualify using current measured EMA9 evidence even when the old absolute BTC extreme is outside its short clock. This retains the original edge identity and paid-attempt rules. A cheaper quote, a phase label, a new route name or a different territory does not by itself create another paid attempt.

Mountain also has an explicit **aligned-leg entry** for a genuinely new native leg, a comparable early-leg location or an eligible opening slice of that leg in a new contract. It requires current native progress, observed origin and fresh evidence, with the existing $0.50 ceiling. An already mature terminal cannot be relabeled as an aligned entry. The exact leg/ticker slice stays consumed after a fill and exit.

Exceptional Release requires actual present directional expansion or sustained separation with current movement. A tiny sign change or an edited label on a flat neutral box cannot steal custody from another qualified tool.

## 2. Carry living legs through ordinary pullbacks

Ordinary collection requires an observed source candle after the entry candle and the applicable minimum age. Wall-clock ageing alone does not complete that guard. The first, second or later internal pause can remain HOLD while the physical leg is supported. There is no instruction to wait for exactly three pullbacks, and a genuinely failed leg can still exit earlier.

A local tactical-entry terminal is now separate from permission to collect a primary. Primary collection requires the earned entry terminal plus either comparable current-leg shape/duration maturity with measured slowdown, or an observed meaningful retreat, retest of the same extreme and fresh rejection. Matching peak gaps plus relative deceleration or a short flat patch alone is insufficient. The distinction uses the actual observed scale: it does not impose a fixed duration, require an EMA curl, or disqualify genuinely small comparable arcs. Mountain's ordinary exit, full-exit handoff and profit policy use the same primary qualifier.

The +100% immediate-arm bypass is removed. +100%, +150% and +600% alone no longer create first protection or authorize tighter new profit bands during a healthy leg. Current qualified physical collection is required. Mountain/Rail/Release retain their baseline 10% band, with the existing 5%, 3% and 2% tiers available when physical collection supports them. A true bounded Heist has its own far-edge collection and 3% band.

Exact per-lot Release custody can preserve a living leg without requiring a global handoff flag to repeat forever. Before a canonical flip, a flat short sample is evaluated with actual post-entry progress and continuing longer-window support. It is not automatically a completed leg. EMA9/EMA30 crosses remain context: an allowed continuation and its original identity do not disappear solely because of a cross.

### A first floor starts from the current executable bid

The executor now separates all-time executable-bid **MFE** from the **protected peak** used after protection is first authorized. To arm a new floor, the current full-lot executable bid must satisfy the arm threshold and produce positive liquidation net after remaining entry basis, allocated entry fees and modeled exit fees.

The initial protected peak is that current bid. An older high cannot create a new floor above the bid now available, nor reappear as a tighter protection peak on the next beat. Already committed floors remain monotonic and binding; accepted partial exits, source/finality obligations and genuine structural failure retain their priority. This correction changes future arming authority, not previously committed live obligations.

## 3. Primary and tactical are independent strategy lots

One normal PRIMARY and one opposite TACTICAL may coexist in the same exact contract, in either booking order and on either side. A first ordinary Mountain edge is primary when no normal primary exists. A separately qualified return beside a primary is tactical. Qualified Rail/Heist overlays retain their bounded tactical role without replacing the parent merely by appearing.

A counter-Rail trade additionally needs favorable observed target-return context or the existing cheap opening-value context. The BTC-to-EMA30 corridor is recorded as observed geometry; it is not treated as a settlement forecast.

Commander, executor and durable reconciliation validate the exact-lot companion proof independently. It binds the roles, distinct physical missions, ticker, generation and current observation. Same-side normal stacking, a repeated role, a third same-ticker lot, stale/malformed proof and a conflicting accepted exit remain blocked. Existing Lazarus coexistence is a separate protocol; a healthy primary does not need false recovery custody to admit a fade.

Each lot keeps its own basis, fees, manager, protected peak, floor and exit reason. Closing or partly closing either role cannot silently alter its companion. Cash and consumed complementary native depth are shared and reconciled. Opposite strategy lots offset aggregate direction; their display does not imply independent venue exposure.

## Current trading constitution

This release intentionally narrows strategic trade authority without deleting the historical modules used by Nautilus, research, or prior forensics.

1. **EMA9 / NAEVYR owns the Mountain leg.** A meaningful living EMA9 Mountain has first trade authority. EMA30 / THAERYON is the broad spine and context only. EMA30 alignment, crossing, residence, or a Rail label cannot select a side, steal a living Mountain, or create an entry.
2. **Mountain entries are terminal EMA9 edges only.** Mid-leg acquisition, opening-slice continuation, full-arc bypasses, and compatible hidden MAIN entries are disabled. A qualified terminal upper edge relays to NO; a qualified terminal lower edge relays to YES.
3. **No second strategic veto after a current Mountain terminal edge.** MissionPolicy, contract thesis, EMA30/Rail context, price-quality grading, ReleaseWatcher, or tactical economics cannot reinterpret a qualified terminal EMA9 edge. Objective PAPER execution checks still apply: current source, open contract, current executable native quote/depth, cash/ledger integrity.
4. **Mountain custody is edge-to-edge.** Numeric profit floors, ordinary slowdown/failure collection, and Release-driven strategy exits do not cut a Mountain leg. The next qualified terminal EMA9 edge is the normal strategy exit/relay. A committed objective safety exit remains independent.
5. **Generic tactical/counter-parent fades are paused completely.** They cannot enter through MissionPolicy, compatible-tool injection, or a full-arc label.
6. **Market Heist is narrow.** It may own only a repeated tight outer-edge box whose current evidence shows compression or parallel EMA behavior. Ordinary directional drift is not Heist.
7. **Rail and Release are telemetry/context only in this release.** Rail can still be displayed as physical context when no living Mountain exists, but no RailTool is activated and no Rail entry is produced. ReleaseWatcher is not attached as a trading overlay and cannot hand off, enter, exit, or transfer territory.

Re-enabling those behaviors merely to make those tests green would violate the current strategy constitution.

The active acceptance gate covers current Mountain/Heist behavior plus the PAPER execution journal, source freshness, settlement/recovery, Windows launcher/export, Nautilus projection, and Looking Glass read-only projection.

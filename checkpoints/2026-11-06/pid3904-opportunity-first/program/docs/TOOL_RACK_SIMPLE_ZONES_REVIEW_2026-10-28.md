## What this revision changes

The governing path is **territory → current entry zone → correct side → hold the purchased leg**. A full mountain peak supplies a NO return entry; a valley supplies YES. Full returns remain blind to the outgoing EMA direction, do not wait for its curl, and do not acquire the 40% screen used for smaller tactical swings. Current executable depth, the existing budget and price ceiling, exact-contract accounting, and genuine source freshness still apply.

Five concrete defects were addressed:

1. **The larger leg was fragmented.** At the opening valley, the chart contained a decline beginning around 01:51, while the live parent tracker measured a roughly three-minute child after a small rebound. The revised source can retain the coherent active leg already present in known completed-minute history. It keeps its origin, extreme and range together, while current received prices supply the entry evidence. Historical minute closes remain separate from received ticks and create no missing weaknesses or terminal events.
2. **The comparison obstacle combined different historical legs.** Independent maximum duration, EMA movement and price range could produce a reference that no actual leg matched. The entry comparison now uses all three dimensions from one actual recent completed leg.
3. **One full zone could face multiple entry decisions.** The direct and compatible-tool paths now share the same full-return opportunity. Native-child confirmation and an earlier consumed local fade cannot veto a separately qualified full-parent zone. The paid full-return opportunity still cannot be purchased twice. A new MAIN continuation needs a real origin/value location or its established continuation proof; young age alone no longer supplies location.
4. **Existing holdings depended on new-entry ownership after gaps.** A purchased ARC can reconnect to a currently observed same-side parent independently of which tool is offering entries. Original position identity, paid cost, frozen boundary, invalidation and missing history remain intact. An unverified holding scope is labelled as unverified.
5. **A local Heist edge could override denied MAIN entry permission.** The completed replay exposed a NO purchase at 03:22:55 even though its source explicitly said `LEG_INTERIOR` and denied current MAIN location. That position sold 4.773 seconds later. The shared policy now requires the source's dedicated MAIN-location permission for every tool. A local box cannot substitute for it. The exact recorded offer and native book now produce WAIT and no ledger entry through the actual Commander and PAPER writer. Full ARC admission is separate and remains blind.

These changes stay inside the standalone Tool Rack. R22 is not imported by the delivered runtime. Nautilus remains included, with the existing narrow feed, larger TV text, enlarged positions, simplified quotes and trade percentages.

## Exit policy for this pass

This revision does not introduce a new percentage target, tighten a trailing rule or redesign losing positions. Ordinary full-leg collection retains the third-distinct-weakness-plus-spent rule. Existing complete-parent handoff, confirmed parent end, profitable contract interruption, committed protection, later-entry-candle restriction, Lazarus and official settlement retain their separate authority.

**Those existing exceptions matter.** A confirmed parent end can collect before a third weakness. In this replay, the first YES exits on its own observed parent end with two weaknesses, after about ten minutes. It is not presented as a third-weakness exit. The source change can alter which physical parent is recognized even though the exit functions and thresholds are unchanged.

# TACHYOS ARCHITECTURE CHECKPOINT — 2026-09-25

## Status

DESIGN / RESEARCH CHECKPOINT ONLY.

This checkpoint records the current architectural direction for Tachys so future runs can be reviewed against a clean target instead of accumulating disconnected observers.

It does **not** grant new paper authority by itself.

---

## 1. Core organizing principle

Tachys should become increasingly geometric and positional rather than a collection of independent YES/NO predictors.

Primary question:

> Where are we inside the current structure, and therefore where is the best place to enter, hold, harvest, defend, or transfer?

Core hierarchy:

- EMA30 = battlefield Spine
- EMA9 = active growing edge / fast geometry
- Leg Geometry = current complete tactical movement
- Zigzag Accountant = sequence of alternating legs and their evolving relationships
- Arc = curvature / local cycle
- Orbit = position inside the larger rotational structure
- Campaign = current larger structural chapter
- Target + Time = settlement geometry and remaining capacity

Direction and entry timing are separate decisions.

A bullish or bearish campaign does not automatically imply an immediate entry.

---

## 2. Conch-shell / Fibonacci organization

Use Fibonacci primarily as a **recursive growth-sequence lens**, not an exact retracement rule.

The shell idea is:

micro movement
-> counter-movement
-> leg
-> counter-leg
-> arc
-> larger rotation
-> campaign

Each layer grows from and contains information from the smaller layer.

Real structures may stretch, compress, deform, become asymmetric, or fail to regenerate.

Do not force 0.382 / 0.618 / 1.618 onto the market.

The Zigzag Accountant may measure these ratios and later test whether any Fibonacci-like clustering appears naturally.

Useful future shell-health labels:

- HEALTHY
- COMPRESSING
- ASYMMETRIC
- DEFORMING
- REGENERATING
- TERMINAL
- NEW_CENTER_FORMING

"Deformity" is information, not automatically noise.

## 3. General Commander

The General Commander receives team reports and creates one War Plan.

The Commander should not be another standalone predictor.

War Plan outputs should include:

- campaign direction / ownership
- current leg direction
- current position inside leg
- parent scale
- target / settlement relationship
- time capacity
- mission type
- contradictions
- preferred action

Preferred actions:

- ENTER
- WAIT
- HOLD
- HARVEST
- TACTICAL_OPPOSITE
- REACQUIRE
- TRANSFER
- STAND_DOWN

The Commander must be allowed to say:

- campaign is UP, but DO NOT CHASE
- parent is intact, but current counter-leg is DOWN
- no parent is reliable; defer campaign and study local legs
- current move is mature; wait for the next favorable position

## 5. Warm-Up / Recalibration phase

Every fresh process should begin with an observer-only warm-up.

Phases:

BOOT
-> CALIBRATE
-> FORMATION
-> WARMUP_READY

Warm-up should announce meaningful changes only:

- BTC/history depth
- EMA9/EMA30 readiness
- 3/6/9 slope agreement
- gap expansion/compression/cross
- target relation
- arc birth / phase change
- first leg state
- zigzag history
- terrain type
- initial battlefield thesis

Warm-up can conclude:

- directional campaign
- transition
- chop / no parent
- insufficient history

For the $666 revival experiment, warm-up remains observer-only initially so the historical strategy comparison is not confounded.

After startup, warm-up becomes a quieter recalibration observer and may reactivate when the existing battlefield model becomes stale or contradictory.

## 8. Historical evidence that remains important

Preserve these cross-run lessons:

- RAID was the most robust initial mission in the sampled runs.
- Campaign should be earned rather than declared immediately.
- ARC_RIDER tactical management added value.
- EARLY_IMPULSE should remain tactical.
- ANTICIPATION is a strong transition/directional sensor but requires parent-scale management.
- TRANSITION_SCOUT often sees direction early but has weak current monetization.
- same-contract re-entry is not globally bad.
- same-contract Campaign re-declaration has been weak.
- management errors have often come from using the wrong structural scale.
- target/settlement invalidation needs richer geometry.
- a dead position may be abandoned; the battlefield is never abandoned.

## 9. Periodic Architecture Checkpoint routine

Repeat this cleanup periodically.

Suggested cadence:
- after a major long run / forensic cycle
- before granting a new observer paper authority
- after approximately 3 meaningful architecture revisions
- whenever console / telemetry becomes noisy enough that the Commander story is hard to follow

At each checkpoint ask:

1. What is still core?
2. What has duplicated another observer?
3. What should be paired or merged?
4. What signal has empirical support?
5. What has failed across multiple runs?
6. What is only descriptive poetry versus measurable geometry?
7. Is any micro observer accidentally controlling a larger scale?
8. Are entries improving position/price, or only improving directional confidence?
9. Are exits respecting leg/parent scale and winner maturity?
10. Is the system becoming simpler to explain?

Checkpoint goal:

> Fewer independent voices, stronger partnerships, one coherent battlefield state, one Commander War Plan.

---

## 10. Current north star

Tachys does not need an endless number of new predictors.

It needs increasingly accurate measurement of:

- EMA structure
- leg position
- zigzag sequence
- arc/orbit position
- target/settlement geometry
- time remaining
- mission identity
- position health

Then:

> enter where geometry offers favorable price,
> hold while the structure retains capacity,
> harvest when the move matures,
> and transfer only when the battlefield itself changes.

This is the current architecture target until a future checkpoint changes it.
